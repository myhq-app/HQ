Thanksgiving Plan

Notes and guesses
  • Why this order: the ham holds heat better than anything else on the menu, so it goes first and rests wrapped for about 1¾ hours. The turkey holds worst, so it cooks last. The mac, potatoes and cornbread share the oven with it, so all of them come out fresh at 1:40.
  • One temperature for everything at the end: the turkey recipe says 350°, so the sides bake at 350° too. The mac takes about 10 minutes longer than your 375° time. Go by how they look.
  • Cornbread bakes first thing at 375° (Krusteaz box: 27–29 minutes), waits under foil and warms up wrapped in foil with the rolls at 1:40. Cornbread reheats well as long as it is wrapped so it does not dry out.
  • Space: three pans on two racks at 1:05. Test-fit them Wednesday.
  • Turkey breast: your video cooked a 4.5 lb breast about 2½ hours. Yours is 48 oz (3 lb), so I used 30 minutes covered plus about 55 minutes uncovered. The thermometer decides: 165° at the thickest part.
  • Ham assumes 11 lb (8 minutes per pound). Tell me the real weight tomorrow and I will move the times.
  • Greens simmer on the stove from 9:00, so the slow cooker is free to keep the yams warm.
  • Rolls use the bag: 3–5 hours to rise, then 350° for 15–20 minutes. If your mom brings them, you get the oven back at 1:40.
  • I do not know about gravy, cranberry sauce or drinks, so they are not on this plan.
